Privacy Policy - Meridian Showbiz Publisher

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Meridian Showbiz Publisher ("the app") is an internal publishing tool used by the owner of the Meridian Showbiz Facebook Page (https://www.facebook.com/MeridianShowbiz) to publish entertainment news posts to that Page. It is not offered to the public and has no public sign-in.

Who uses the app. Only the Page owner and any people the owner explicitly adds as app roles.

Data we process. Through the Facebook Graph API the app uses the Page identifier, a Page access token (stored in encrypted repository secrets and never published) and the content of the posts it creates. The app does not collect personal data from Page visitors, followers or commenters, and it does not read private messages.

How we use it. To publish and verify our own posts, prevent duplicate posts, and keep redacted operational logs that contain no access tokens.

AI-generated images. Illustrative images are created by a third-party AI image-generation service from a text prompt that describes an abstract illustration. Prompts contain no personal data. Posts are labelled as AI-generated.

Sharing. We do not sell personal data. Publishing automation runs on GitHub.

Retention. Operational records (post IDs, status, timestamps) are kept while the Page is active.

Your choices and contact. To ask a question or request deletion of any data relating to you, send a message to the Meridian Showbiz Facebook Page (https://www.facebook.com/MeridianShowbiz). You can also remove the app's access in your Facebook settings under Apps and Websites.

Changes. We will update this page when our practices change.
