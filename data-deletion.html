Data deletion instructions - Meridian Showbiz Publisher

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The app does not store personal data about Facebook users other than the Page owner and authorised role holders.

To request deletion of any data relating to you, send a message to the Meridian Showbiz Facebook Page (https://www.facebook.com/MeridianShowbiz) with the subject "Data deletion". We will confirm and act on the request as soon as reasonably possible.

You can also remove the app yourself in Facebook: Settings and privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites > Meridian Showbiz Publisher > Remove.
